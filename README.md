## 💻 Why Back-end?

Back-end development really speaks to me because it’s where the “magic” happens behind the scenes. Designing scalable and efficient back-end services challenges me to solve real-world problems and think deeply about how apps handle loads and complexity.

While the front-end is what users see, the back-end is the foundation that makes it all possible—and that’s where I want to make my impact. Learning Python, TypeScript, SQL, Docker, and CI/CD has opened my eyes to how powerful well-built back-end systems can be, and I’m excited to keep growing these skills as I work on meaningful projects.

For me, back-end development is not just about coding—it’s about creating reliable, maintainable solutions that people and businesses can depend on every day.
